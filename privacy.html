Privacy Policy

MPLH 26 : Match3 3D · Developer: MPLH Game
Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how the game and its related website handle information. It applies to the game distributed through supported stores and to the official promotional website at this address.

1. Overview

MPLH Game publishes MPLH 26 : Match3 3D as a paid game. You do not need to create an MPLH Game account to play. The core gameplay is designed to work without sending your level progress to an MPLH Game-operated online account or server.

This policy describes the core game implementation. Device operating systems, app stores and any additional services included in a particular store build may process information under their own policies. MPLH Game will review this policy whenever a build changes.

2. Game data stored on your device

The game stores settings and progress locally using Unity's on-device preferences/storage mechanisms. Examples include:

  • Unlocked level and other gameplay progress or results saved by the game;
  • Music and sound-effect on/off preferences and volume levels;
  • Other preferences required for the game to function.

These values are not requested as an MPLH Game account profile. The core implementation does not upload these local preferences to an MPLH Game-operated game server. Depending on your device settings, operating-system backup services may independently back up application data.

3. Paid purchases and storefront services

The game is sold through third-party digital storefronts, where available. Purchase transactions, billing information, receipts, payment authentication and refunds are handled by the relevant storefront and payment provider under their own terms and privacy notices. MPLH Game does not ask players to enter payment card details directly into the game.

Examples of platform operators include Google Play, Apple's App Store and Microsoft Store. They may also collect device, purchase or diagnostic information independently of the game.

4. Social Links and voluntary sharing

The game may open external sites when you select Social Links, Privacy Policy, or Share. Sharing gameplay results is optional. Depending on the option you choose, the game may prepare text describing your result and open an external sharing website or copy a message to your device clipboard.

MPLH Game does not automatically post to your social account. When you leave the game or open an external site (such as X, Facebook, WhatsApp or the MPLH Game Social Links page), the external service's own privacy policy applies.

5. Website, cookies and hosting

This promotional site is hosted using GitHub Pages (GitHub's privacy statement). The website files provided by MPLH Game do not include advertising scripts, analytics trackers, account login forms, or first-party tracking cookies.

GitHub and your browser/network providers may process technical request details, including IP addresses, for hosting, security and operations under their own privacy notices. External links may apply their own tracking or cookies after you visit them.

6. Support and communication

If you choose to contact MPLH Game by email, the messages and personal details you include (such as your email address and issue description) will be used to answer your request and handle support. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card numbers or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

7. Data retention, deletion and your choices

  • Local game data: remains on your device unless cleared through applicable device settings, removed by uninstalling the app, or affected by device backup/restore behavior. Uninstalling may not delete separately retained device backups.
  • Support emails: retained only as reasonably needed to address your inquiry and maintain necessary operational or legal records. You may ask MPLH Game to review or delete correspondence where applicable.
  • Store transaction records: controlled by the relevant storefront subject to its retention rules; contact that store for payment/receipt requests.

Because the core game does not provide an MPLH Game account, there is no game account to close or remote progress profile to delete.

8. Children's privacy

The game does not require players to disclose their names or register a profile. We ask anyone contacting support to avoid sending personal information about children. Parents or guardians can contact MPLH Game using the email below with privacy questions. Store age ratings and family settings are provided by the relevant platforms.

9. Security and changes to this policy

MPLH Game aims to minimize personal information processed by the game. No online service or transmission is completely risk-free; external website and platform protections are controlled by their respective operators.

This policy may be updated when game functionality, included third-party SDKs, platform distribution or legal requirements change. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.
